Privacy notice

Last updated: 11 November 2026.

Who is responsible

Florian Triclin, publisher of mindyard.dev, is the data controller. Contact: hello@mindyard.dev.

What is collected, and why

Only through the waitlist form, and only if you submit it:

  • your email address;
  • the price bracket you chose, if you answered that optional question;
  • the date of your sign-up and the version of the consent text you accepted.

The single purpose is to tell you when a hosted version of mindyard opens, and to gauge whether there is enough interest to build it. Your address is not used for anything else, not shared, not sold, and no newsletter is sent.

Legal basis

Your consent (Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR), given by ticking the box in the form. You can withdraw it at any time; withdrawal does not affect what was done before.

How long it is kept

Until the hosted version is announced to you, and in any case no longer than 12 months after your sign-up. It is then deleted.

Where it is stored

In a database on the server that hosts mindyard.dev, rented from OVHcloud in the European Union. Nobody else has access to it.

Server logs

Like any web server, the server records each request (IP address, date, page requested, browser) in its logs, for security and troubleshooting. These logs are deleted after 14 days. The waitlist itself does not store your IP address.

No cookies, no tracking

mindyard.dev sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or third-party scripts.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by writing to hello@mindyard.dev. If you think your rights are not respected, you can lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL.
